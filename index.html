Beat the DM. Grow their tab, not yours.

You and the table play WAR against the Drink Master. Beat the DM and your rivals pay for it. Lose, and the DM drinks you under the table. Finish with the lowest Drink Tab to win.

3–6 players 15–25 minutes Ages 16+ Drinking optional

Every round, seven steps

Each player picks a suit and plays a 25-card deck. Your hand is always four cards, topped back up after every round and every War.

  1. The DM reveals the top card of the DM deck.
  2. Double Down now if you want to (optional).
  3. Everyone plays 1 card from their hand, face down.
  4. Draw back up to 4 cards.
  5. Reveal all played cards at once.
  6. Resolve the round.
  7. Anyone at 4 Shot Tokens takes a shot. Anyone who couldn't play is eliminated.

Resolving the round

  • Didn't beat the DM? Take 1 Shot Token. Beat it and you take nothing.
  • Lowest card of all players? Take 1 more.
  • Matched the DM, or tied another player? That's a War.
  • The highest card that beats the DM wins the round and uses its Suit Ability.

Card ranks, low to high: Buzz-Kill (0), 2 through 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace. The game ends when the DM deck runs out (everyone −2 Drink Tab), when only one player is left (that player −1 Drink Tab), or when every player is eliminated (the DM wins, everyone +2 Drink Tab). Either way, the lowest Drink Tab wins.

Six suits, six ways to play dirty

Every player picks a suit, and every suit comes with its own Suit Ability. It triggers only when you win the round and beat the DM.

Hearts

The Survivor

Pass 1 of your Shot Tokens to any player, or reduce your Drink Tab by 1.

Diamonds

The Schemer

Look at one player's hand and swap 1 card of your choice with them, or remove 1 Shot Token from yourself.

Clubs

The Pressure Engine

All other players gain +1 Shot Token. In 5–6 player games, one player of your choice gains +1 more.

Spades

The Executioner

Target player gains +2 Shot Tokens. Won with a face card? +1 more.

Mugs

The Tank

Gain 1 Shield Token (max 3). Each Shield cancels 1 Shot Token you would receive.

Bottles

The High Roller

Double your rewards this round as if you had declared Double Down, or target player gains +2 Shot Tokens.

When cards collide, it's WAR

Wars are fought with cards from your hand, for at most 2 rounds. Draw back to 4 afterward.

Player vs Player

Each player in the War plays 1 card face down, then everyone flips. Highest card wins. Tied? Each plays a second card. Tied again? It's a draw, with no winner, no loser, no penalty and no reward.

The loser takes +2 Shot Tokens. The winner takes nothing.

Player vs DM

The DM puts its top card down face down, and each player in the War plays 1 card. Flip everything. You only fight the DM's card, not each other.

Beat it and you take −1 Drink Tab. Lose and you take +1 Shot Token. Tied? The DM draws a second card and you play a second one. Tied again, and nothing happens.

Double Down

Declare right after the DM reveals, before anyone plays. That round, everything you gain or lose is doubled, War included. A lost Player vs Player War costs you +4 tokens. Win the round and remove 2 Shot Tokens or take −1 Drink Tab. Win a War vs the DM and take −2 Drink Tab.

Suit Abilities are never doubled.

Buzz-Kill

Play yours as a card worth 0 and sit out the round's results. Afterward, remove up to 2 Shot Tokens or reduce your Drink Tab by 1, then discard it.

When the DM reveals one, every player without a Buzz-Kill in hand takes +1 Shot Token. Players holding one discard it, take no token, and draw back to 4.

Drinking is optional

The game plays exactly the same with Shot Tokens and no drinks at all. At 4 Shot Tokens you take 1 shot, add 1 to your Drink Tab, and return the 4 tokens. Shields cancel tokens automatically.

Keep score on the Drink Tab pad. Make a tally for each shot taken, and the lowest total at the end wins.

The drinking add-on is only for adults of legal drinking age where you're playing. For everyone else, a "shot" can be soda, juice or water. Please drink responsibly.

A look at the deck

Every player deck has 18 number cards (2–10, two of each), 2 Jack of Beer, 2 Queen of Rum, 1 King of Wine, 1 Ace of Buzz and 1 Buzz-Kill. Here's one suit, Hearts, from seven up to the Ace.

The Drink Master's deck

24 ranked cards, one for every rank, with 1 Buzz-Kill per player shuffled in at setup.
