The whole table vs. the Drink Master.

A fast tavern battle card game. Beat the Drink Master, make everyone else drink, and try to end the night with the lowest tab. Lose, and the DM drinks you under the table.

How it plays See the cards
Every round

Four steps, no downtime

You and the table are playing WAR against the Drink Master. Everyone's hand is four cards, and every round is decided at once.

01

Reveal the DM

The Drink Master's card is flipped in the center. That's the number to beat.

02

Play face-down

Everyone picks one card from their hand, then draws back up to four.

03

Flip together

All cards are revealed at once. One highest card beats the DM and every player.

04

Pay the price

Everyone who didn't win takes a shot token. The lowest card takes two.

When cards collide

Ties go to WAR

Match the DM, or match each other, and the round stops being polite. Tied players build a four-card war stack and flip it out.

WAR!

Player vs. Player: tied players flip their war stacks, highest card wins, and the losers take three shot tokens.

Player vs. the DM: beat the DM's war card and you get to hand a token back. Fail, and everyone in the war takes two more shots.

Buzz-Kill

The Joker. It's worth zero and can't win a war, but play it in a normal round and you skip that round's penalties entirely.

Watch the DM deck, though. Every Buzz-Kill shuffled in there is a chance for the Drink Master to turn the table's own tricks on them.

Keeping score

The lowest tab wins the night

Shot tokens pile up fast. Hit the limit and they turn into a real drink on your tab, and the tab is what decides the game.

5 tokens= 1 real shot, plus 1 on your drink tab.
0 cardsEmpty deck and empty hand and you're out, with two shots for the road.
Lowest tabWins. You can beat the DM for the table, but the real champ drank the least.

House rule: want a rowdier night? Make 3 tokens equal one real drink instead of 5.

A peek at the deck

Drink-suited royalty

Number cards run 2 through 10, then the tavern court takes over: Jack, Queen, King, and the Ace of Buzz.
